Our Story

From Dongguan Craftsmanship to a Hunan Smart Factory

CHAMWIN was established on September 5, 2022 by Dongguan Changying Plastic Woven Packaging Co., Ltd. As costs rose in the Pearl River Delta, we brought our two decades of woven packaging expertise inland — building a brand-new, ¥206 million, 103.1-acre manufacturing campus in the Rucheng Economic Development Zone at the junction of Hunan, Guangdong, and Jiangxi provinces.

The result: Dongguan-grade quality with the capacity, cost efficiency, and modern facilities that today's global food and feed brands demand. Our products already serve Yihai Kerry, COFCO, China Resources Wufeng, and Taili — and now we're opening our production lines to overseas buyers worldwide.

Capabilities

One Factory, the Full Production Chain

Every step in-house — from raw resin to finished, printed, sewn bags ready for container loading.

🧵

Tape Extrusion

Virgin PP resin extruded into high-tenacity tape and drawing — the foundation of bag strength and food-contact safety.

⚙️

Circular Weaving

Modern circular looms running around the clock, producing consistent fabric from fine 10kg food bags to heavy 50kg sacks.

🖨️

Printing & Lamination

Up to 8-color flexo printing and BOPP film lamination — vivid, smudge-proof branding that survives long supply chains.

Cutting & Sewing

Automated cutting with reinforced single/double stitching and heat-seal bottoms, verified by drop testing.

Quality Control

Export Logistics
